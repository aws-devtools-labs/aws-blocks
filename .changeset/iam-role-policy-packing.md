"@aws-blocks/blocks": patch

The shared execution role's statements are now merged before CDK packs any overflow into managed policies: statements with the same effect and conditions (and no `Sid`, `NotAction` or `NotResource` differences) that share their actions or their resources are combined, never both lists at once, so no permission is added. The role's overflow managed policies now get logical IDs derived from their contents (`BlocksOverflowPolicy<hash>`), and the role's default policy depends on them, so when a deploy moves statements between the role's policies, a statement is not removed from its old policy before its new policy is attached (CloudFormation otherwise updates those policies in parallel; see aws/aws-cdk#35611). IAM is eventually consistent, so a newly attached policy can still take a few seconds to apply. Because the default policy depends on every overflow policy, making a resource depend on one specific grant to the role (for example `grant.applyBefore()` or a grant's `policyDependable`) while the role is also granted access to that resource can make CloudFormation report a circular dependency; make the resource depend on the whole role instead, or use a separate role.

Upgrading changes the overflow policies' logical IDs (from `...OverflowPolicyN` to `BlocksOverflowPolicy<hash>`) and their generated names once. Anything keyed on the old logical IDs, such as stack policies or template tests, must be updated. For an app whose role already overflows, the first deploy after upgrading replaces each overflow policy, creating the new one before deleting the old one.

When the role's grants are packed into overflow managed policies and the role would then carry more managed policies than the per-role quota allows, synth now fails with an error naming the role and the Building Blocks that grant the most (roles without overflow policies are not checked). The `@aws-blocks/core:managedPoliciesPerRoleQuota` context raises the limit (up to 20) for accounts with an increased quota. Synth also warns when the role is within the quota but a deploy that replaces every overflow policy would briefly exceed it, since the old and new copies are both attached during that deploy.
