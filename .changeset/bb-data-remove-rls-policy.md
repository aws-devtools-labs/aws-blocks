Remove the unused `rlsPolicy` option from `DatabaseOptions` and from the `Database` block generated by `db pull`. The option had no effect at runtime: `db.crud()` behaves the same with or without it. If an earlier `db pull` wrote `rlsPolicy: 'enforce'` into your database wiring file, delete that line, or re-run `db pull`. Also corrects the production-setup text in the generated `MIGRATION_GUIDE.md`, which referred to an option that does not exist.
