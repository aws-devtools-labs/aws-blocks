# Billing and refunds

We charge your card on the first day of each monthly cycle. An invoice is emailed
to the account owner whenever a payment succeeds.

To request a refund, open the billing page and choose the charge you want
reversed. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within five to
seven business days.

If a payment fails we retry it twice over the following week before the
subscription is paused. You can update the card on file at any time from the
billing settings.
