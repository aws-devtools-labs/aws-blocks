# Shipping and delivery

Standard delivery takes three to five business days once an order leaves the
warehouse. We ship on weekdays only; orders placed over the weekend go out the
next Monday.

A tracking link is emailed as soon as the carrier scans the parcel. You can
follow the shipment from the orders page until it is marked delivered.

We currently ship to addresses within the continental United States. Expedited
two-day shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee.
